Identity for the BRAC Sir Fazle Hasan Abed Knowledge Hub, created at Loudworks. Flowing parallel lines suggest knowledge in motion, set against a calm palette of deep green and warm sand. The system runs from stationery and ID cards to notebooks, badges, apparel and tote bags.
